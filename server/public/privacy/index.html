Alokchitri helps you set up your phone's camera for better photos. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your phone

  • The live camera view and light readings are used on your phone to measure light. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Your saved recipes, chosen language, phone name and daily feedback count are stored only on your phone.

What is sent to our server (only when you ask for photo feedback)

  • The photo you pick, reduced in size, together with its saved camera settings (ISO, shutter speed, aperture, focal length, flash), your phone's name and your chosen language.
  • Our server passes these to an AI model to write feedback about your photo, and returns the answer to the app. The model may run on our own computer or on Anthropic's AI service.
  • We do not keep your photo or the feedback. The server writes only an error message to its log if a request fails.

Ads and purchases

  • The free version shows an optional rewarded ad through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details.

Permissions

  • Camera: to measure light.
  • Display over other apps: to show the settings checklist over your camera app.
  • Notifications: to show that the checklist is running and let you close it.

Your choices

You can use the app without asking for photo feedback; then nothing is sent to our server. Uninstalling the app deletes all data stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
